Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Euclid Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Euclid, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. This classification requires the most stringent cleanup protocols, including full personal protective equipment, containment barriers, removal of all porous materials that contacted sewage and thorough disinfection of every remaining surface. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

We clear the standing sewage first, then pull contaminated water out of structural materials, remove carpet, padding, insulation, drywall and any other porous item that was in contact with sewage for proper disposal, disinfect each hard surface, treat with antimicrobial products and finish with structural drying, which prevents secondary mold. We also deodorize the affected area with industrial-grade hydroxyl generators and thermal fogging equipment to eliminate lingering sewage odors.

Why Sewage Cleanup Is Not a DIY Job

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Handling raw sewage safely takes specialized training, EPA-registered disinfectants that consumers cannot buy, and personal protective equipment, meaning respirators, Tyvek suits and protective boots and gloves. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Contaminated materials count as biohazard waste, and disposing of them properly is part of a professional cleanup. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Hair and other debris are easy to stop with simple strainers on tub, shower and sink drains
  • Stormwater from a sump or downspout should never drain into the sewer, since the extra volume invites a backup
  • Protect against municipal sewer backups by having a backwater valve fitted where your line connects
  • Clear tree roots near sewer lines, which can infiltrate and block the pipes
  • Every 2 to 3 years, get a sewer camera run through the line to spot developing issues
  • In an emergency a plumber needs the cleanout, so learn where yours is and keep it in good shape

Warning Signs for Sewage Cleanup

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Several fixtures backing up at once instead of only one
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Toilets or drains that gurgle whenever you run water somewhere else
